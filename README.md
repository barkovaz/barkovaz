# Barkovaz

I make free digital content with AI assistance.

I hate AI slop, and I want my work to show the other side. AI in good hands, used with good intent,
lets anyone make good things, even with zero coding knowledge.

## How I work

### Everything I make is AI-assisted

My standards are high. I never settle for the first thing the AI makes: I keep shaping everything until
it looks and works the way I envision it, with a focus on visual and behavioral consistency and
immersion. I check every release in my own test scenarios, but I can't be 100% sure something works
everywhere, every time. If something breaks, tell me and I'll fix it.

### Everything I make is free

No paid versions, no early access, no content locked behind donations. Donations only help me spend
more time on this.

### All my source code is public

Everything is on GitHub, and every release lists the SHA-256 checksums of its files, so you can confirm
that what you downloaded is exactly what I published ([how to check](VERIFYING.md)).

### Anyone can build on it

All my code is under the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/license/mit): use it, learn from it, fix
it and make your own variants. Credit and a link back are appreciated.
